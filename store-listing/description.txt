Ratings for Netflix

See IMDb-sourced ratings right on Netflix's title preview card — no more tabbing away to look
something up before you press play.

HOW IT WORKS
On first use, you point this extension at IMDb's official public dataset files (the links are
pre-filled for you). It downloads them directly into your own browser, builds a local ratings index,
and never sends your data anywhere. After that first setup, ratings appear instantly while you browse
— no network requests, no accounts, no tracking.

FEATURES
- IMDb rating + vote count shown on the hover preview card
- Optional color-coding by score (green / amber / red)
- Optional dimming of tiles rated below a threshold you choose
- Everything runs locally; nothing about your Netflix browsing is collected or transmitted
